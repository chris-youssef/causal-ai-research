# Causal AI Research

Undergraduate Research | Causal Inference | Machine Learning | Python | PyTorch | Pyro

## Overview

This repository documents my ongoing undergraduate research and exploration of Causal Artificial Intelligence (Causal AI) at the University of Miami.

My research focuses on investigating the applications of causal inference and machine learning to Parkinson's disease progression, with an emphasis on understanding causal relationships between variables rather than relying solely on statistical correlations.

The broader objective is to explore how causal modeling techniques can contribute to understanding complex medical conditions and support data-driven biomedical research.

## Research Areas

- **Causal Inference:** Investigating cause-and-effect relationships beyond traditional statistical correlations.
- **Structural Causal Models (SCMs):** Studying mathematical frameworks for representing causal relationships between variables.
- **Directed Acyclic Graphs (DAGs):** Exploring graphical representations of causal structures and dependencies.
- **Causal Interventions:** Understanding how interventions can be used to investigate causal effects.
- **Probabilistic Programming:** Exploring probabilistic modeling approaches using PyTorch and Pyro.
- **Machine Learning in Healthcare:** Investigating potential applications of machine learning to Parkinson's disease progression.

## Technologies & Tools

- **Programming:** Python
- **Machine Learning:** PyTorch
- **Probabilistic Programming:** Pyro
- **Development Environment:** Jupyter Notebook
